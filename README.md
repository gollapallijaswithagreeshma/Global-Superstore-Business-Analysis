# 📊 Global Superstore Business Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes the **Global Superstore** dataset to identify sales trends, profitability, product performance, regional performance, discount impact, and shipping-cost patterns.

The analysis was performed using **SQL and Tableau**, with exploratory data analysis used to understand the dataset and identify important business insights.

---

## 🎯 Business Objective

The main objective of this project is to answer key business questions such as:

* How are sales and profit changing over time?
* Which categories generate the highest profit?
* Which regions and categories perform poorly?
* Which products generate the highest and lowest profits?
* How do discounts affect profitability?
* Which products have high shipping costs?
* Where can the business improve profitability?

## 📊 Key Insights

### 1. Sales Growth

Sales increased consistently from **2011 to 2014**, indicating strong overall business growth.

### 2. Category Performance

**Technology** generated the highest total profit among the major categories, while **Furniture** had a considerably lower profit margin.

### 3. Regional Performance

Regional profitability varies significantly across categories. Some region-category combinations generate strong margins, while others produce losses.

### 4. Discount Impact

Higher discounts can negatively affect profitability, particularly for products that already have relatively low margins.

### 5. Product Profitability

A small group of products contributes significantly to overall profitability, while several products generate substantial losses.

### 6. Shipping Costs

Some products have relatively high shipping costs compared with their sales, which can reduce overall profitability.

---

## 💡 Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis:

1. **Review high-discount products** and evaluate whether discounts are generating sufficient additional sales.
2. **Investigate loss-making products** and consider pricing, sourcing, or discontinuation strategies.
3. **Focus on high-margin categories and products** to improve overall profitability.
4. **Review high shipping-cost products** and explore opportunities to optimize logistics.
5. **Analyze low-performing region-category combinations** and develop region-specific strategies.
6. **Monitor profitability rather than sales alone** when making business decisions.
